Our Mission

Delivering our team's experience, resources and service excellence to families, endowments, foundations and corporations across the U.S., Europe and Latin America.

Because the future is never certain

We believe that unnecessary risk is something no one can afford — that's why we help people craft financial plans that are sensible, comprehensive, and research-based. We founded the firm on two inherent beliefs that continue to shape our approach today: we hold ourselves out as your trusted resource, whether you're our client or not, and we always keep your best interests as our only concern.

We truly work as a team, not just a group of individuals — our experience is vast. We were there for Tech-Wreck 2000, 9/11, the Great Recession of 2008, and helped navigate COVID, emotionally and in the markets. Through each, we've helped multigenerational families, non-profit organizations, and high-profile athletes and entertainers build and maintain the pillars of their financial lives. We want to help you change your life.

We work with people we like

That's our rule. It's why our client base represents such a diverse array of executives, families, business owners, nonprofit organizations, professional athletes, and entertainers — good people who value responsible advice, that's who we want in our professional family.

Some clients are just around the corner from our offices; others are in large U.S. and international cities, or on the road for the majority of the year. Some have been with us for four generations, others for less than one — but no matter how far away you are, or how long you've been our client, we strive to be your ever-present financial resource by keeping our practice intentionally intimate.

A financial plan made for you

You deserve a solid financial future, and that starts with a sound financial plan — one that reflects who you are, where you are, and where you want to be 10, 20, or even 50 years down the line. After we've laid your financial foundations, we pause to ask: "What's it all for?"
